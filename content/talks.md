## Selected Talks

**Architecture Under Constraints: Designing Systems That Still Evolve**\
_PyCon DE & PyData 2026_

Most systems are not built on a clean slate. They have legacy, regulation, organizational boundaries, and decisions that cannot easily be undone.

In this talk, I look at how to make architectural decisions when those constraints dominate the design space: distinguishing real constraints from assumed ones, designing around capabilities, preserving future options, and making trade-offs that remain explainable over time.

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/rFd8ghVirv8?si=RC0FaLK-gTN-Ltyg) · [PyCon DE talk Page](https://2026.pycon.de/talks/ZLRFR9/)

**Why AppSec Fails at Scale (and How to Fix It)**\
_OWASP Global AppSec Vienna 2026_

Application security rarely fails because teams do not care about security. More often, it fails because security practices do not scale with the number of developers, systems, and decisions an organization has to make.

This talk looks at how to move AppSec away from individual heroics and reviews towards paved roads, secure defaults, fast feedback, and controls that become part of how engineering works.

The recording is currently available to OWASP members only. I will add the public recording here once it is officially released.

Watch on YouTube (soon) · [OWASP AppSec talk Page](https://owaspglobalappseceuvienna20.sched.com/event/2JGou/why-appsec-fails-at-scale-and-how-to-fix-it)
